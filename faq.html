Frequently Asked Questions

Help, tips, and guides for WakeUp.

How to import custom alarm sounds to WakeUp

  1. Locate the audio file on your computer, or find it in your Music app.
  2. If using the Music app, right-click the track and select Show in Finder.
  3. Copy the audio file (.m4a, .mp3, or .wav) to an iCloud Drive folder, or send it to your iPhone or iPad via AirDrop.
  4. On your device, open WakeUp → Settings → Custom Sounds, tap Choose Audio File…, and select the file from the Files app.
Note: You must own the audio file you import. Only DRM-free audio (such as music you have purchased or your own recordings) is supported. Protected tracks from streaming services cannot be imported.

I am traveling to a different time zone. What should I keep in mind?

When you travel across time zones, keeping your alarms on schedule is simple:

  • Regular repeating alarms automatically follow your device's local time.
  • Alarms with temporary exceptions (such as an adjusted next alarm or a skipped occurrence) are scheduled in Apple's AlarmKit using a fixed point in time. In the app, these are marked with a pin icon.

What to do: When you arrive in a new time zone, simply open WakeUp. The app automatically detects your new time zone and updates your alarms to local time.

Showing the time zone: If an alarm is scheduled in a time zone that differs from your current location, its time zone is automatically displayed next to the pin icon. If you want to always display the time zone next to the pin, turn on WakeUp → Settings → Changing time zones? → Show Time Zone Details.

Tip: WakeUp displays a brief confirmation once your alarms have been updated for your new time zone.

What are alarm categories and how do I use them?

Alarm categories help you group and organize your alarms by routine or purpose (such as Work, Morning, Exercise, Medication, or Weekend):

  • Assign a category: When creating or editing an alarm, tap Category and choose the category that fits. You can also assign categories to multiple alarms at once using Bulk Edit Alarms from the top menu (•••).
  • Filter the alarm list: Once you assign categories to your alarms, a filter bar appears at the top of the main screen. Tap any category icon to instantly show only those alarms, or tap the grid icon to view all alarms.
  • Manage categories: Open the menu (•••) at the top of the alarm list and select Alarm Categories to see how many alarms belong to each category and quickly adjust their memberships.
Tip: Filtering by category keeps your alarm list tidy and lets you focus on just the alarms you need for work, workouts, or everyday routines.

How can I sort my alarms?

You can customize how your alarms are ordered in the alarm list by tapping the menu button (•••) in the top corner of the main screen:

  • Alarm Time: Orders alarms chronologically by their set time (default).
  • Next Alarm: Orders alarms by whichever will ring next in countdown order.
  • Category: Groups alarms according to their assigned category (available when categories are in use).
  • Name: Sorts alarms alphabetically by their label.

Advanced Sorting: In the same menu, tap Advanced Sorting… to configure two-tier sorting with primary and secondary criteria (such as sorting by recurrence first, then by alarm time) along with ascending or descending order.

Tip: Sorting by "Next Alarm" is especially useful when managing multiple alarms, keeping the soonest alarm right at the top of your list.

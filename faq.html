Will my alarm ring if my device is on silent, in Do Not Disturb, or the app is closed?

Yes, absolutely. WakeUp is powered by Apple's official AlarmKit framework, which schedules your alarms as full system alarms:

  • App closed: Your alarms will always ring on time whether WakeUp is open, running in the background, or completely closed. You do not need to keep the app open overnight.
  • Silent mode & mute switch: System alarms override silent mode, ringing with audio even when your device is silenced.
  • Do Not Disturb & Focus modes: Alarms ring normally even when Do Not Disturb, Sleep Focus, or any other Focus mode is active.
Important: Make sure alarm permissions are enabled on your device (Settings → WakeUp → Alarms) so WakeUp can schedule reliable system alarms.

How do Live Activities and Dynamic Island work with WakeUp?

WakeUp integrates directly with iOS Live Activities and the Dynamic Island via AlarmKit and ActivityKit, allowing you to monitor active alarms and snooze countdowns at a glance without unlocking your iPhone:

  • Dynamic Island: Displays a compact alarm icon and real-time countdown at the top of your screen. Touch and hold the Dynamic Island to expand it and view detailed alarm controls.
  • Lock Screen & Always-On Display: Shows a clean, live banner with the active alarm status and exact remaining snooze countdown right on your lock screen.
  • Automatic Activation: Starts seamlessly when an alarm alerts or is snoozed, so you always know exactly how much sleep time is left.

How to ensure Live Activities are enabled: On your iPhone, open Settings → WakeUp and verify that Live Activities is switched on. Additionally, check Settings → Face ID & Passcode and ensure Live Activities is enabled under Allow Access When Locked.

Tip: You can interact with or monitor snoozed alarms directly from the Lock Screen banner or expanded Dynamic Island without having to open the app.

How does Dismiss Next Alarm work?

Dismiss Next Alarm lets you skip only the upcoming occurrence of a repeating alarm (for example, if you woke up early or have a day off) without altering your regular schedule:

  • Dismissing an alarm: As the alarm time approaches, a Dismiss button (crescent moon icon) appears on the alarm row. Tapping it skips the next alarm, and WakeUp automatically returns to your regular schedule afterward.
  • Restoring an alarm: If your plans change, tap the Next alarm is dismissed button that appears on the alarm row to restore the alarm immediately.
  • Button visibility window: In the alarm editor, you can adjust Show Dismiss Button to control how many hours before the alarm (2, 4, 8, 12, or 24 hours) the Dismiss button appears on your screen.
Tip: You never have to turn off a repeating alarm and remember to turn it back on – dismissing only affects the single next occurrence.

How does Quick Adjust (+/-) work?

Quick Adjust lets you move only the next occurrence of a repeating alarm earlier or later without changing your regular schedule:

  • Adjusting the time: When an alarm is set for today or tomorrow, - and + buttons appear on the alarm row. Tap them to nudge the upcoming alarm earlier or later step by step.
  • Viewing and restoring: The adjusted offset (such as -10 min or +20 min) appears as a badge on the alarm row. Tap this badge anytime to instantly restore the alarm to its original time.
  • Automatic reset: Once the alarm rings, WakeUp automatically returns to your regular schedule for all future occurrences.
  • Adjustment Step: In the alarm editor, you can customize the Adjustment Step to change the increment to 5, 10, 15, or 30 minutes (10 minutes is default).
Tip: Quick Adjust is ideal when you need 15 minutes of extra sleep tomorrow or have to get up slightly earlier without altering your recurring routine.

I am traveling to a different time zone. What should I keep in mind?

When you travel across time zones, keeping your alarms on schedule is simple:

  • Regular repeating alarms automatically follow your device's local time.
  • Alarms with temporary exceptions (such as an adjusted next alarm or a skipped occurrence) are scheduled in Apple's AlarmKit using a fixed point in time. In the app, these are marked with a pin icon.

What to do: When you arrive in a new time zone, simply open WakeUp. The app automatically detects your new time zone and updates your alarms to local time.

Showing the time zone: If an alarm is scheduled in a time zone that differs from your current location, its time zone is automatically displayed next to the pin icon. If you want to always display the time zone next to the pin, turn on WakeUp → Settings → Changing time zones? → Show Time Zone Details.

Tip: WakeUp displays a brief confirmation once your alarms have been updated for your new time zone.

What are alarm categories and how do I use them?

Alarm categories help you group and organize your alarms by routine or purpose (such as Work, Morning, Exercise, Medication, or Weekend):

  • Assign a category: When creating or editing an alarm, tap Category and choose the category that fits. You can also assign categories to multiple alarms at once using Bulk Edit Alarms from the top menu (•••).
  • Filter the alarm list: Once you assign categories to your alarms, a filter bar appears at the top of the main screen. Tap any category icon to instantly show only those alarms, or tap the grid icon to view all alarms.
  • Manage categories: Open the menu (•••) at the top of the alarm list and select Alarm Categories to see how many alarms belong to each category and quickly adjust their memberships.
Tip: Filtering by category keeps your alarm list tidy and lets you focus on just the alarms you need for work, workouts, or everyday routines.

How does the next alarm highlight and countdown timer work?

WakeUp can automatically highlight the next alarm scheduled to ring in your main alarm list and display a live countdown showing exactly how much time remains (for example, Next · 7 hr 25 min):

  • Visual Highlight: Marks the row of the upcoming alarm with a dedicated accent badge so you can identify it at a glance.
  • Live Countdown: Displays the remaining sleep time in hours and minutes, updating smoothly every minute.
  • Dynamic & Smart: If you dismiss the next occurrence, adjust it earlier/later, or pause an alarm, WakeUp immediately recalculates and highlights the next active alarm.

How to enable or disable it: Open WakeUp → Settings → Alarm List and toggle Highlight Next Alarm (indicated with a star icon ⭐️). When turned on, the badge appears directly under the alarm time in your main list.

Tip: The countdown automatically accounts for Daylight Saving Time transitions and time zone differences, giving you an exact, dependable count of remaining hours and minutes until your alarm sounds.

How does pausing a repeating alarm work?

Pausing allows you to temporarily suspend a repeating alarm for a specific date range (such as vacations, holidays, or business trips) without disabling the alarm entirely:

  • Scheduling a pause: Open the editor for any repeating alarm, expand Advanced Settings, and turn on Pause Alarm. Choose your desired start and end dates from the calendar.
  • Viewing pause status: The alarm list displays clear status indicators: Pause scheduled before the pause begins, and Paused through [date] while the pause is currently active.
  • Automatic resumption: Once the pause period ends, WakeUp automatically reactivates the alarm according to its regular recurring schedule.
  • Editing or cancelling: You can adjust the dates or cancel the pause at any time by opening the alarm editor and turning off Pause Alarm.
Tip: You never have to worry about forgetting to turn your morning alarm back on after a vacation – WakeUp automatically resumes right on schedule.

How do I change the time input method (Time Editor)?

WakeUp offers three distinct ways to set your alarm time, letting you pick whichever input method feels most comfortable for you:

  • Scroll Wheel: The familiar iOS scrolling wheel picker (default).
  • Number Pad: Quickly type the exact alarm time using numbers.
  • Clock Dial: An intuitive, visual round clock face where you set the time by turning the hands.

How to change it: Open WakeUp → Settings → Time Input (under Alarm Defaults) and choose your preferred editor. Your choice will be used whenever you create or edit alarms.

Tip: You can switch between input methods at any time in Settings to see which one feels quickest and easiest for you.

How can I sort my alarms?

You can customize how your alarms are ordered in the alarm list by tapping the menu button (•••) in the top corner of the main screen:

  • Alarm Time: Orders alarms chronologically by their set time (default).
  • Next Alarm: Orders alarms by whichever will ring next in countdown order.
  • Category: Groups alarms according to their assigned category (available when categories are in use).
  • Name: Sorts alarms alphabetically by their label.

Advanced Sorting: In the same menu, tap Advanced Sorting… to configure two-tier sorting with primary and secondary criteria (such as sorting by recurrence first, then by alarm time) along with ascending or descending order.

Tip: Sorting by "Next Alarm" is especially useful when managing multiple alarms, keeping the soonest alarm right at the top of your list.

How to import custom alarm sounds to WakeUp

  1. Locate the audio file on your Mac, or find it in your Music app.
  2. If using the Music app, right-click the track and select Show in Finder.
  3. Copy the audio file (.m4a, .mp3, or .wav) to an iCloud Drive folder, or send it to your iPhone or iPad via AirDrop.
  4. On your device, open WakeUp → Settings → Custom Sounds, tap Choose Audio File…, and select the file from the Files app.

Windows and other cloud services: You can also import audio files from a Windows PC. Simply transfer the file to your device using any cloud service (such as iCloud.com, Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox) or send it to yourself via email, and save it to the Files app.

Note: You must own the audio file you import. Only DRM-free audio (such as music you have purchased or your own recordings) is supported. Protected tracks from streaming services cannot be imported.

What are the requirements and limits for custom alarm sounds?

WakeUp allows you to personalize your alarms with your own audio files. Here are the key rules, supported formats, and sound management features:

  • Import Limit: You can import up to 20 custom sounds. If you reach the limit, delete an existing sound before importing another one.
  • Supported Formats: AIF, AIFF, CAF, M4A, MP3, and WAV (must be DRM-free audio files).
  • Automatic Shortening & Fade-Out: Sounds longer than 30 seconds are automatically shortened to 30 seconds, with a smooth 2-second fade-out applied at the end for an optimal alarm experience.
  • Preview & Renaming: In Settings → Custom Sounds, tap any sound to preview it or tap and hold to rename it.

Safe Deletion (Sound in Use): You can delete sounds at any time by swiping. If you delete a sound that is currently assigned to one or more alarms, WakeUp safeguards your alarms by prompting you to choose a replacement sound so your alarms never ring silently.

Tip: All imported sounds are converted and stored locally on your device in Apple's optimized audio format, ensuring fast playback and zero network dependency.

How do I set the default alarm sound or update all alarms at once?

WakeUp allows you to choose a default alarm sound for all newly created alarms, and even update every existing alarm in your list with a single tap:

  • Setting a default sound: Open WakeUp → Settings → Default Sound (under Alarm Defaults) and select your preferred built-in sound, custom audio file, or System Default.
  • Updating all existing alarms: In the sound picker, switch on Apply to All Existing Alarms and tap Use (or Apply). WakeUp will update every alarm in your list instantly without needing manual editing.
  • Individual customizations preserved: If you leave the toggle off, your new default sound will only apply to future alarms you create, leaving existing alarms unchanged.

Sound options: You can select any of WakeUp's bundled sounds, your imported custom sounds from My Sounds, or the iOS system default.

Tip: If you ever want to change sounds for just a few specific alarms rather than all of them, use Bulk Edit from the main alarm list menu instead.

How do I edit multiple alarms at once (Bulk Edit)?

Bulk Edit allows you to update settings across multiple alarms simultaneously without editing each alarm individually:

  • Open Bulk Edit: Tap the menu button (•••) in the top corner of the alarm list and select Bulk Edit Alarms.
  • Select your alarms: Choose the alarms you want to modify, or tap Select All to select all alarms at once.
  • Choose what to change: Toggle on only the settings you wish to apply:
  • • Change Alarm Sound: Apply a new built-in or custom imported sound to all selected alarms.
    • Change Snooze Duration: Set a uniform snooze length (5–30 minutes).
    • Change Category: Assign the selected alarms to a category or clear their category.
    • Change Adjustment Step or Dismiss Button: Customize quick adjust increments or dismiss button visibility.
  • Save changes: Tap Apply to update all selected alarms immediately.
Tip: Bulk Edit is especially useful when you want to change the alarm sound for an entire category of alarms or adjust snooze durations across your whole schedule in seconds.

How do I adjust the snooze duration or disable snooze?

Unlike Apple's built-in Clock app, which fixes snooze to exactly 9 minutes, WakeUp gives you full control over snooze duration for each individual alarm, or lets you turn it off completely:

  • Custom Snooze Duration: Choose any duration from 1 to 30 minutes using the stepper in the alarm editor.
  • Disable Snooze: Turn the Snooze toggle off if you want your alarm to ring without a snooze option, helping you wake up immediately.
  • Bulk Editing: You can also update snooze settings across multiple alarms simultaneously using Bulk Edit in the top menu.

How to adjust it: Tap an alarm to edit it, scroll to the Snooze section, ensure the Snooze toggle is on, and use the Snooze Duration stepper (+/-) to set your desired minutes.

Tip: When an alarm is snoozing, WakeUp shows the live countdown in the Lock Screen Live Activity and Dynamic Island, so you always know exactly when it will alert again.

How do I change the app appearance (Dark Mode or Light Mode)?

WakeUp is designed to look great in both dark and light environments. By default, it follows your iPhone's system-wide appearance, but you can override it specifically for WakeUp at any time:

  • System Default: Automatically matches your iOS appearance and changes with your day/night schedule or Control Center toggle.
  • Light: Keeps WakeUp in a clean, bright appearance regardless of your device's mode.
  • Dark: Locks WakeUp into an eye-friendly, OLED-optimized dark theme, ideal for checking alarms at bedside in a dark bedroom.

How to change it: Open WakeUp → Settings → Appearance (under General) and select your preferred theme. The screen includes a live preview so you can see how the interface looks before leaving settings.

Tip: Dark mode is especially gentle on your eyes in low-light environments when setting or adjusting your alarm before going to sleep.

What languages does WakeUp support, and how do I change the language?

WakeUp is fully localized into 6 languages. By default, it automatically matches your iPhone's system language, but you can also choose a different language specifically for WakeUp at any time:

  • English
  • Finnish (Suomi)
  • Swedish (Svenska)
  • German (Deutsch)
  • Spanish (Español)

How to change the language: Open WakeUp → Settings → General → Language and pick your preferred language. You can also select System Default at any time to automatically follow your iOS system settings.

Tip: Changing the language in Settings instantly updates the entire app interface, alarm labels, weekdays, and Live Activities without needing to restart your device or the app.

Privacy Policy for WakeUp

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Data Collection

WakeUp does not require an account and does not collect personal data such as your name, email address, location, or contacts.

Alarm settings, categories, and any imported audio files are stored locally on your device as required for the app to function.

Optional anonymous usage analytics. If you choose to allow it, WakeUp collects anonymous product interaction data to help us understand how the app is used and improve it. This includes, for example, which features are used (such as creating alarms, sorting, filtering, bulk editing, pausing, skipping the next alarm, quick time adjustments, importing a custom sound, or using categories), approximate alarm-count ranges, and app settings such as language, appearance, and time input method.

Alarm names, alarm times, and sound names are never collected, and the data is not linked to your identity.

Analytics is off until you choose. You can allow or decline when the app asks, and change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy. When you turn analytics off, collection stops and the analytics identifier on your device is reset.

Third-Party Services

If you allow analytics, WakeUp uses Google Firebase Analytics, a service provided by Google, which processes the anonymous usage data on our behalf. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy.

WakeUp does not use third-party advertising services, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA), and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Data Sharing

WakeUp does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties. The anonymous usage data described above is used only for analytics and is not linked to your identity or used for tracking.

Children's Privacy

WakeUp does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality or data practices of WakeUp change. Any updates will be published on this page.

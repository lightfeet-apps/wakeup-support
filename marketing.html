WakeUp

An alarm clock designed for changing plans.

Change tomorrow without changing your regular schedule.

Your schedule stays the same. Tomorrow doesn't have to.

WakeUp lets you dismiss the next occurrence of a repeating alarm or fine-tune its time without changing your regular weekly schedule.

Sleeping in tomorrow or already awake? Dismiss the next alarm with one tap.

Need to wake up earlier or later just once? Move the next alarm in 10-minute steps. WakeUp automatically returns to your normal weekly schedule afterward.

Know exactly what's next

See when your next alarm will ring and how much time remains.

WakeUp keeps your upcoming alarm clear so you always know what is scheduled next.

Features

  • Dismiss the next occurrence of a repeating alarm
  • Move only the next alarm earlier or later
  • Adjust the next alarm in 10-minute steps
  • Automatically return to your regular schedule afterward
  • See the next alarm time and how long remains until it rings
  • Create one-time and repeating alarms
  • Select individual days, weekdays, weekends, or every day
  • Choose from seven alarm sounds created for WakeUp
  • Import your own AIF, AIFF, CAF, M4A, MP3, or WAV audio
  • Choose a different sound for each alarm
  • Set the default sound for new alarms
  • Adjust the snooze duration from 1 to 30 minutes
  • Choose between 12-hour and 24-hour time
  • Start the week on Monday or Sunday

Available in five languages

WakeUp is available in English, Finnish, German, Spanish, and French.

Follow your device language or choose the app language separately.

Built for real life

Keep regular mornings predictable and make changes when you need them—without disabling an entire alarm or remembering to restore your schedule later.

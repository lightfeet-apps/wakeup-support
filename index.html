Troubleshooting & Good to know

Cannot create or save alarms (Missing permission): WakeUp schedules system alarms using Apple's AlarmKit. If alarm permissions have not been granted, the app intentionally prevents creating or saving alarms so you never end up with an alarm that cannot ring. To enable it, open your device's Settings → WakeUp → Alarms and allow permissions.

Changing time zones (Repeating alarms with exceptions): Repeating alarms with temporary adjustments (such as Quick Adjust, Dismiss Next Alarm, or Pause) may be scheduled in Apple's AlarmKit at a fixed point in time and do not update in the background on their own. When you arrive in a new time zone, simply open WakeUp once and your alarms will automatically update to your new local time.

Alarm volume (Sound is too quiet or device only vibrates): WakeUp uses your device's native system ringtone and alert volume. If your alarm sounds too quiet, adjust the volume slider in Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone and Alerts. (Tip: If 'Change with Buttons' is turned on in iOS, the volume may have been turned down inadvertently using the physical side buttons).

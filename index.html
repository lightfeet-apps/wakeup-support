Good to know

Alarm permissions: WakeUp uses Apple's AlarmKit to schedule reliable system alarms. To ensure alarms always ring on time even when the app is closed, make sure alarm permissions are enabled in Settings.

Changing time zones: Repeating alarms with temporary exceptions (such as an adjusted time or a skipped alarm) are scheduled in Apple's AlarmKit using a fixed point in time. When changing time zones, simply open WakeUp and your alarms will update automatically to your new local time.
